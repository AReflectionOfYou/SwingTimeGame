The idea

Each phone is a handbell with four notes. A shared screen (iPad, laptop, or a TV through AirPlay) shows one colored lane per phone. Blocks slide toward a white line; when a block in your color reaches the line, make the swing its arrow shows.

Getting started

  1. On the shared screen, open Host a game, pick a song and the number of phones.
  2. Each player scans the code in their color with the iPhone camera.
  3. On the phone, tap Enable Motion and allow it.
  4. The host presses Start. After 3, 2, 1 the score starts moving.

Scanned the wrong code? Just scan the right one.

How to hold the phone

Hold it like a hatchet or a hammer — gripped sideways, not upright as if reading it.

  • Right hand: the screen's left edge points down.
  • Left hand: mirrored — the right edge points down. Pick Left hand at the bottom of the player screen.

The four swings

ArrowSwing
↑Swing forward — a firm chop forward and down
↓Swing back — bring it sharply back up
←Snap left
→Snap right

Your phone shows which note each swing plays. Tap a note to hear it without swinging.

Toddler mode

Any wave at all plays the next note of the tune, so it always comes out right. To leave, a grown-up presses and holds the ✕ in the corner for two seconds.

Can't hear it?

  • Press volume up while the page is open.
  • Check the ringer switch on the side of the phone.
  • If headphones or a Bluetooth speaker are connected, the sound goes there.
  • Tap a note on the player screen to test.

Swings misfire?

Open Debug for a live sensor readout, a log of detected swings, and sliders for the sensitivity thresholds.
